AI Workbench Ingestion

A private utility that synchronises authorised source material into the owner's Google Drive workspace for personal knowledge management.

Not a public service. This application is operated for the owner's personal use and does not offer accounts, subscriptions or access to other users.

Purpose

The application captures and reconciles authorised source records, currently including Plaud transcripts and summaries, into a controlled Google Drive source archive.

Controls

It uses a dedicated Google identity, encrypted credential storage, stable source identifiers and application checks that restrict processing to its configured source folder.

Data use

Information is used only to operate the owner's personal AI Workbench. It is not sold, used for advertising or made available as a public dataset.

Privacy and support

The Privacy Policy explains the Google Drive information accessed by the application, why it is used, where it is stored and how access can be revoked.
